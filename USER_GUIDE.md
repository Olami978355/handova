# User Guide

Welcome to Rent Easy! Find your role below to get started.

---

## 🙋‍♂️ For Tenants
**Objective**: Find a safe place to live or land to buy without being scammed.

1. **Search**: Use the map or list view to browse properties.
2. **Shortlist**: Click the Heart icon to save properties you like.
3. **Chat**: Use the "Chat with Owner" button to ask questions.
4. **Book Inspection**: Pay the small commitment fee (~N2,000) via Paystack to schedule a viewing.
5. **Safe Deal**: If you like it, pay the full price through our Escrow. We hold the money until you confirm you've moved in.

---

## 🏠 For Landlords & Agents
**Objective**: List your properties and manage tenants professionally.

1. **Get Verified**: Submit your NIN and/or LASRERA ID in your profile settings. Verified owners get 3x more views.
2. **List Property**: Click "List Property" and follow the steps. Be sure to upload clear photos.
3. **Manage Inspections**: You will receive notifications when a tenant books an inspection. Confirm the time and meet them at the site.
4. **Escrow Status**: Track your payments in the "Deals" tab. Funds are released once the tenant confirms or the grace period ends.

---

## 🛡️ For Admin & Staff
**Objective**: Maintain the community standards and safety.

1. **Verification Queue**: Review incoming NIN/LASRERA documents in the Admin Panel.
2. **Dispute Resolution**: If a tenant claims an inspection didn't happen, use the dispute queue to refund the tenant or release to the landlord.
3. **Property Moderation**: Remove listings that violate community guidelines or look suspicious.
4. **Auditing**: Use the Admin Logs to track system-wide changes.
